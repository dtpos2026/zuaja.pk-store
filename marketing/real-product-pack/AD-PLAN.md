Plan yeh hai: ek naya **"Real Product"** ad set banayein, ya pehle se chalti campaign mein in ads ko add karein. 7 din mein dekhein kaunsa creative jeet raha hai, phir budget usi par lagayein.

## 2. Inspection notes (read before running)
- **Real photos win trust.** They are hand-held, real bottles, real packaging. Lead with them in **cold audiences**.
- **The White Oud box contains a Signature bottle in the footage** (clip C078, photos IMG_2005/2006). In ads I only use the box *front* for White Oud and the *open box* for Signature. **Fix this in real orders too.**
- Label typo on the Haldi bottle ("CLEANYSES"): it is small but visible in close-ups. Fix it on the next print run.
- Night photos were graded: product brightened and sharpened, background darkened. Faces and hands are kept natural (UGC style).

## 4. Test plan
- **Days 1–3:** do not touch anything (learning phase).
- **Day 3:** compare **CTR (link)**, **cost per landing page view** and **cost per add-to-cart** for:
  - real photo vs studio creative of the same product;
  - **commercial music vs aura music** (same video, two ads);
  - **UGC reel vs branded reel** (the UGC style usually wins on cold audiences).
- **Day 4:** pause losers (CTR < 0.8 % after Rs 600 spend). Move winners into the main CBO campaign and raise the budget +20 % every 48 h.
- **Every week:** refresh with new real clips. Phone videos of unboxing, application and texture are the cheapest high-performers.

## 5. Copy rules used
- First line = hook + keyword ("Real rosemary inside", "Gift-ready luxury").
- Soft claims only ("helps", "stronger-looking"); no medical promises.
- CTA **Shop now**, with the link going to the product page and UTM tags attached.
